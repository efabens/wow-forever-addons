# GuildWave validation

- Run `make validate-forever-addons` from the repository root. Expect both Lua files to parse and the discovery and action checks to pass.
- Run `make install-guildwave` from the repository root and confirm `GuildWave.toc` sits directly inside `_forever_/Interface/AddOns/GuildWave`. Start Forever, enable GuildWave and friendly nameplates, then confirm the panel appears without Lua errors.
- Confirm the GuildWave launcher toggles the panel on left-click and opens its options on right-click. With MinimapButtonButton enabled, open its collection and verify the launcher retains both actions after collection. Use `/mbb list` to check for `GuildWaveMinimapButton`.
- Right-click the window header and the background. Confirm each opens the view menu and the current view is selected. Right-click a player row and confirm it opens the emote menu. Drag the window to a new position, then watch players enter and leave range. Verify the title bar stays in place while the bottom edge moves. Near the bottom of the screen, use the mouse wheel to reach the remaining rows.
- Stand beside a guildmate and another player outside the guild. Target that other player, then click the guildmate's row. Confirm chat shows a wave directed to the guildmate, the selected target stays the same, and GuildWave shows no error for the successful wave.
- Choose Anyone from the window menu and confirm the friendly non-guildmate appears. Choose Other Guild, open `/guildwave options`, and enter their full guild name using **Guild name → Edit**. Verify the filter and an action from the row. Open the editor again to check the saved value. Cancel an edit, then save an empty name and confirm the list becomes empty. Reenter the name, reload, and confirm the filter is restored. Choose Guild to return to your own guild.
- In Anyone, turn friendly player nameplates off and check that `/guildwave debug` reports them off. Click **Enable friendly player nameplates** while out of combat. Run the command again to confirm the setting is on and check counts for exposed nameplates and matching players.
- Run `/guildwave debug` while a target is selected, clear the target, then run `/reload`. In the character's `SavedVariables/GuildWave.lua`, confirm `GuildWaveDB.debugReports.manual` describes the target status and `GuildWaveDB.debugReports.logout` records the later state.
- Enable **Pin selected target to top** in Options. Select a player outside the current filter and confirm the Target row appears at the top. Select a player already in the list and confirm they appear once, with room for the full configured number of other players. Repeat with an NPC and check for a Target row with no Whisper action. Clear the target and verify that row disappears.
- Right-click the guildmate. Try Fart, Whistle, and Chicken, and confirm each performs its corresponding game emote. Then choose Whisper and confirm the draft opens with the guildmate's name.
- Out of combat, right-click a nearby guildmate and choose **Target**. Confirm the game selects that player and updates the pinned row if enabled. Run `/guildwave debug` and check for `nameplate | selected`. Repeat on a party or raid row with friendly nameplates off and check for `group | selected`. Reopen the menu and verify a click outside or Escape closes it. Check that **Right-click menu: Target** toggles the option. Open the menu and enter combat to verify that it closes. Reopen it in combat and confirm Target is disabled. Repeat after combat and check that selecting Target works again.
- If a Target click leaves the selection empty, run `/reload` and inspect `debugReports.logout.targetAction`. Check the command, combat flag, observed macro and `TargetUnit` calls, any client error, and the immediate and delayed results.
- Change the Guild left-click action, disable an action in its right-click menu, and set Maximum names to 1. With multiple guildmates visible, verify the list limit and the configured actions. Drag the panel, reload, and confirm the settings and position persist.
- Use Options to set different left-click actions in all four views. Switch among the views and click available rows to confirm each uses its own action. Reload and confirm the choices persist.
- Open the menu on a guildmate, then have them leave nameplate range. If the menu stays open, select an emote and confirm no action is sent. Have a second player move into view and confirm actions apply only to the player selected.
- Join a party and switch to Party/Raid. Confirm nearby members appear, your character is excluded, and clicking a name dances. Turn off friendly nameplates and verify a nearby party member still appears when the client exposes the range check. Repeat in a raid and check that the view keeps its own settings.
- In combat and in a restricted instance, confirm the panel handles hidden identities and unavailable actions without Lua errors or a blocked-action popup. The live client is required to confirm which units and emotes it exposes in those situations.
